Municipal Coverage

6 Intelligent Problem Classifications

Our natural language processing pipeline classifies citizen descriptions across 6 core urban civic categories with confidence scoring.

Road Damage
Potholes, cracks, asphalt erosion

Detects craters, tar peeling, road subsidence, and bridge hazards. Auto-prioritized by traffic risk and pedestrian danger.

Water Leakage
Pipeline bursts, potable leaks

Identifies drinking water main line fractures, high-pressure geysers, contamination alerts, and community faucet wastage.

Garbage Collection
Overflowing bins, dump piles

Monitors uncollected wet waste, public market debris, illegal chemical dumping, and dispatches municipal compactors.

Streetlight Failure
Outages, sparking poles, dark roads

Surfaces blackout corridors, dangling live wires, defective timers, and electrical hazards to guarantee public safety.

Drainage Problems
Open manholes, sewage overflow

Flags critical open manholes, clogged storm culverts, waterlogged intersections, and residential sewage backflows.

Public Infrastructure
Broken benches, damaged bus stops

Manages parks, vandalized seating, broken pedestrian foot overbridge railings, and damaged pavement tiles.
